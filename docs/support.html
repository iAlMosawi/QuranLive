Frequently asked questions

Why isn't my microphone working?

Allow microphone access in your browser or iOS settings. Reload or restart the recitation session and check that another application is not using the microphone.

Why aren't words highlighting?

Speak clearly, check your microphone permission and wait for the recognition model to download on first use. On the website, server-side fallback recognition may be used when local recognition is unavailable.

Where are my bookmarks?

Reading progress and bookmarks are stored locally, not synchronized through an account. Clearing site data or changing devices may remove or hide them.

Why won't a surah load?

Check your internet connection and reload the page. The website fetches Quran text from the server.

Contact support

Open a support request on GitHub. Include the device model, OS/browser version, steps to reproduce and an optional screenshot. Please do not share private information or audio recordings publicly.
