سياسة الخصوصية

How Quran Live handles microphone access, recitation audio and reading preferences.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Microphone and recitation audio

Microphone permission is requested when you use live recitation tracking. On the website, recognition normally runs on your device. If on-device recognition cannot start, short audio clips may be transmitted to the application's server for fallback recognition. The project states that these clips are processed in memory and not stored. The iPhone app uses Apple's speech recognition; its processing may vary by OS version and available recognition mode.

Reading preferences

Bookmarks, reading progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account. Clearing local storage or deleting the app may remove this information.

Website requests and technical information

The website retrieves Quran content and announcements from the server. Hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps and browser details for service delivery and security. The exact retention periods depend on the hosting configuration.

Advertising and analytics

According to the project's published description, Quran Live has no advertising or user tracking. This policy will be updated if those practices change.

Your choices

You can revoke microphone permission in browser or device settings. You can remove locally stored preferences using the relevant browser or device controls.

Children

Quran Live does not require registration. Parents and guardians can manage device microphone permissions.

Contact and updates

For questions, contact us through GitHub Issues. Do not include personal information or audio recordings in public issues. This policy may be updated as the app changes.
