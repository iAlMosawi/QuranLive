Quran Live

القرآن المباشر

Read the Quran while the app follows your recitation, word by word.

Quran Live is an Arabic right-to-left Quran reader with all 114 surahs in Uthmani script (Hafs). The app highlights words as you recite and helps you continue your reading.

Live recitation

Follow words as you read aloud, with pause and resume.

Read and explore

Surah search, juz and Madani page navigation, bookmarks and saved progress.

Personalize reading

Font-size controls and dark mode. Web access and an iPhone/iPad app.

Quran Live is a reading and practice aid, not a tajweed grading tool. Features may differ between web and iPhone versions.
